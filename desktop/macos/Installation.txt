Swarmview — Your agents. One view.

1. Drag Swarmview.app onto Applications in this window.
2. Open Swarmview from Applications, not from the mounted installer.
3. Once the installed app is running, you can eject the installer and move the
   downloaded DMG to Trash. The installed app stays in Applications.

After a successful launch from Applications, Swarmview offers to eject the
matching mounted installer and move its download to Trash. Choose Keep Installer
to leave it in place.

Updating an existing copy? Quit Swarmview before replacing it in Applications.

This open-source prototype is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. If macOS blocks the
downloaded app, try opening it once, then use System Settings > Privacy & Security
> Open Anyway, if offered, and confirm that you trust the download. Do not disable
Gatekeeper globally. Building from source remains available.

Requires macOS 13.3 or later. Ghostty terminal activation may request Automation
permission. The app does not install a privileged service, launch-at-login item,
or background updater. Quitting it also stops its bundled monitoring server.

Source, documentation, and issue reports:
https://github.com/yukinoda/swarmview
